  • Telecom market share

    Which operators gain or lose subscribers, and why

    MTN’s voice share rose from 49.01% to 52.08% and it won 59% to 71% of port-ins each quarter. EMTS (9mobile) lost the most ground, with net porting of −17,611 over the period.

    Power Query, MySQL, Power BI

    The question. Which mobile operators are gaining or losing share, where, and what does number porting say about why subscribers switch?

    What I did. Cleaned seven quarters of regulator data (Q2 2024 to Q4 2025) in Power Query as part of a team of four, answered 14 business questions in MySQL using CTEs and window functions, and built a one-page Power BI dashboard.

    Code and documentation on GitHub

  • Staple food prices

    A four-item basket against national food inflation

    Headline food inflation was +16.96% year on year, while the four-item staple basket fell 24.7%, with the fall concentrated in two items.

    Excel, Power Query, star-schema model

    The question. Does headline food inflation show what staple foods really cost food and consumer businesses?

    What I did. Built a star-schema model in Excel with Power Query from published price and inflation data (November 2024 to May 2026), checked every calculation against the published figures and logged each cleaning decision.

    The basket covers four commodities, so it is a cost signal for planners and not a replacement for official inflation.

    Code and documentation on GitHub

  • Financial wellness

    What I did. Designed the survey and a 0 to 100 Financial Wellness Score across budgeting, saving, emergency funds, investing and financial literacy for 308 respondents. Cleaned and explored the data in Python, queried it in SQL and built a three-page Power BI dashboard.

  • Credit risk portfolio

    A credit bureau view for a Nigerian lender

    The generated portfolio shows a 33.3% default rate, 63.15% in Rivers State, and 785 pre-default customers with high utilization and debt burden.

    Python, SQL, Power BI

    The question. Where is a lender’s credit portfolio most exposed to default?

    What I did. Generated 10,000 customer records in Python, cleaned them (median imputation for the 3% to 4% of missing values), answered 12 SQL questions and built an executive Power BI dashboard.

    The data is synthetic, so the default rate is not a benchmark for the Nigerian lending market.
